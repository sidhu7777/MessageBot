Simple Connection Diagram 

Internet
  |
  v
Nginx :443/:80
  |
  v
FastAPI app :8000
  |
  +--> /telegram/webhook/{key}
  |      -> route/account resolution
  |      -> session/FSM
  |      -> Kafka turn queue
  |      -> ChannelDelivery reply to Telegram
  |
  +--> /whatsapp/webhook
  |      -> inbound normalization
  |      -> session/FSM
  |      -> reply via WhatsApp provider
  |
  +--> /whatsapp/web
  |      -> booking web UI
  |      -> AJAX calls to /whatsapp/web/*
  |
  +--> /qr/checkin
  |      -> patient check-in page
  |      -> submit to /qr/checkin/submit
  |
  +--> /qr/generate
         -> builds QR SVG
         -> QR encodes /qr/checkin?doctor_id=...&clinic_id=...
